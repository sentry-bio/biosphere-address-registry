# Biosphere Address Registry Constitution

BAR records scientific evidence without promoting a model output beyond what
its measurement supports.

## Ten clauses

1. **Sequence identity first.** Every observation is anchored by a cryptographic
   digest of normalized sequence bytes and a declared normalization policy.

2. **Distance before coordinates.** The primary map reading is a neighborhood
   of named references under a declared metric. Coordinates and projections
   are derived views.

3. **Evidence before verdict.** Rank evidence, neighbors, support, typicality,
   and quality are retained. A taxonomic call is a replaceable interpretation.

4. **Three clocks.** Form, map, and taxonomy versions are independent. Changing
   one never silently mutates the others.

5. **The model is replaceable.** An encoder is identified by the effective
   composed-weight digest. An index may serve only when its recorded encoder
   identity matches the loaded encoder.

6. **The index is a map, not the datum.** A reference index is a map edition.
   TriangleCCS Form is the slowly changing frame. The Addressability Limit is
   the mathematical boundary, not a serving dependency.

7. **Organisms occupy regions.** A read maps to a point-like observation; a
   genome is a distribution of windows; a species is a distribution of
   genomes. A displayed pin is a summary with dispersion, never the object.

8. **Chart honesty.** Kappa and dimension on Form are conventions. Radius is
   advisory. Chart origin is not LUCA. Theta is candidate until an independent
   conformance gate passes. A visualization projection is not the frame.

9. **Metric honesty.** Angular cosine, Poincare geodesic, Mash, JC69, and
   patristic distance are distinct instruments. A field named `distance`
   always carries a `metric_id` through its enclosing record.

10. **Append, do not rewrite.** Published objects are content-addressed and
    immutable. Corrections, reclassifications, and new map readings append new
    records linked to those they supersede.

## Forbidden promotions

BAR must not silently infer:

- species truth from nearest-neighbor retrieval;
- genealogy from endpoint packing;
- curvature from contrastive training;
- dimension from a curvature equation;
- accumulated information from radius;
- certification from warm-start agreement;
- tree fidelity from a tree reconstructed from the same distances;
- uncertainty from an uncalibrated score;
- stable coordinates from an unstamped model/index pair.

## Status vocabulary

Public values use one of:

`THEOREM | IDENTITY | CONVENTION | INSTRUMENT | EMPIRICAL | CANDIDATE |
ADVISORY | OPEN | CIRCULAR`.

The status belongs to the assertion, not the file containing it.
